BiteNewsBot

BiteNewsBot reads public RSS feeds so that Bite, a news reader, can show a short summary of a story and then send the reader to your site to read it.

How to identify it

Every request Bite makes carries this exact User-Agent. It is fixed. It is never rotated, never varied per request, and never disguised as a browser.

BiteNewsBot/1.0 (+https://waterduckpani.github.io/Bite/bot)

What it does

  • Reads your RSS feed every six hours, and takes only a handful of items per run.
  • Respects robots.txt for every request, including the feed itself, and honours Crawl-delay. Requests to the same host are spaced by at least one second regardless.
  • Fetches an article page only when your robots.txt allows it, and only when your feed did not already include the article text. If your robots.txt disallows our crawler, Bite uses your feed's description and nothing else.
  • Writes a summary of at most 80 words, capped in code. The summary is designed to inform, not to replace: it says what happened and why it matters, then points the reader at you.
  • Always shows your name and links to your page. Publisher attribution and the original URL are required fields — a Bite card cannot be displayed without them.

What it does not do

  • It does not bypass paywalls, login walls, consent walls, or bot protection. If a page is not served to an honest, identified client, Bite does not have it and falls back to your feed's description. Nothing is ever retried with different headers.
  • It does not impersonate a browser or use any other User-Agent.
  • It does not republish your articles. No article body is ever rendered inside the app, from any source — tapping a story opens your own page, with your layout, your branding and your advertising.
  • It does not crawl your site. It reads your feed, and at most one article page per story it selects.

Removal

To have your publication removed, email bitenewsapp@gmail.com with your domain.

Removal is a single switch. It stops all fetching from your site on the next cycle and immediately removes your existing stories from every reader's feed. No justification is needed and none will be asked for.

Blocking us yourself

Adding this to your robots.txt stops the article fetch immediately — Bite will fall back to your feed's own description:

User-agent: BiteNewsBot Disallow: /

If you would also like your feed left alone entirely, email us — a robots.txt rule covering your feed path is honoured too, but telling us is faster and we would rather hear from you.

Traffic reporting

Bite records how many times your stories were shown and how many readers clicked through to you. If you would like those numbers for your domain, ask and we will send them.
