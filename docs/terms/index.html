1. What Bite is

Bite shows you a short, automatically written summary of a news story and then sends you to the publisher's own website to read it. It is a referrer, not a replacement. Bite does not host, republish or reproduce anyone's articles: every story opens on the publisher's page, with their layout, their branding and their advertising.

Bite is an independent personal project run by Bharat Khanna. It is free, it carries no advertising, and it sells nothing. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a partner of any publisher whose feed it reads.

2. The summaries

The short "bite" on each card is generated by an automated language model from the publisher's own feed description or article text. It is capped at 80 words.

Automated summaries can be wrong. They can misstate a fact, lose a crucial qualifier, or read a headline more confidently than the story supports. A summary is a signal about whether to read the article — it is not the article, not a substitute for it, and not a source you should rely on, cite, or act on. Where the summary and the publisher's article disagree, the publisher's article is correct and the summary is not. Bite gives no warranty as to the accuracy, completeness or timeliness of any summary.

Bite does not endorse the content it links to, and the selection of stories is produced by an automated ranking system rather than editorial judgement.
